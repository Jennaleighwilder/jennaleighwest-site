About

Founder. Lead Engineer. Systems Architect.

Jenna Leigh West is the founder and lead engineer behind WRENTECH, a custom systems and software studio building websites, AI tools, automations, intake flows, dashboards, and business infrastructure.

The work is simple: find the bottleneck, build the tool, make the system easier to run.

She came to technology through necessity, pattern recognition, and a refusal to accept broken systems as permanent. Her background spans early childhood education, anthropology, sociology, private childcare, legal and real-estate support networks, AI research, and full-stack build work.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Click any question to open the answer.

What do you actually build?

Websites, landing pages, intake systems, client portals, dashboards, AI helpers, automations, document workflows, and custom software tools. The common thread is practical infrastructure for businesses that need the work to move faster.

How long does a software build take?

Rapid sites and intake tools can move in days. More custom software or AI builds depend on scope, integrations, data, revisions, and whether the business already has usable content. Every build starts with a diagnostic so the timeline is honest.

How much does it cost?

WRENTECH publishes flat-fee lanes: diagnostic/audit work starts around $497, rapid sites start around $1,500, market intelligence reports start around $3,500, business builds commonly land in the $3,500-$8,000 range, and larger software or AI systems are scoped per project.

Do you work with neurodivergent clients?

Yes. Neurodivergent clarity is part of the build philosophy: plain language, clear hierarchy, less noise, predictable flows, and systems designed for how real people actually think under pressure.

What is The West Method?

The West Method is Jenna's pattern-recognition and systems-mapping framework. In the WRENTECH lane, it shows up as fast diagnosis, careful question design, workflow mapping, AI prompt architecture, and tools built around human behavior instead of generic templates.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. I sign NDAs for all software development and sensitive consulting work. Confidentiality is standard, not an add-on. What you share with me stays with me.

Can you audit what I already have?

Yes. A diagnostic can review your current site, SEO/GEO visibility, customer path, lead capture, forms, CRM handoff, AI readiness, content structure, and where the operation is losing time or revenue.

Can you build AI-powered applications?

Yes. AI integration is a core part of the work: custom assistants, intake analysis, document processing, research tools, governed workflows, prompt architecture, and practical automations that do a specific job.

Do you offer ongoing support after a project?

Yes. For software: I offer ongoing maintenance, updates, and support packages. For consulting: I offer follow-up sessions and check-ins. Nothing I build is meant to be handed off and forgotten — it is meant to work, and if it stops working, I want to know.

Where are you based? Do you work remotely?

I am based in rural Tennessee and work entirely remotely with clients across the US and internationally. Time zones are not a barrier. I respond within 24 hours on business days.

What systems do you decode — what does that mean exactly?

Business, legal, medical, educational, financial, institutional, creative, and digital systems. A system is any set of rules, relationships, tools, documents, and handoffs that shapes what people can or cannot do. I read the structure, find the leverage, then build or explain the next move.

How do I get started?

Fill out the contact form, email wren@wrentech.net, or start at wrentech.net. Tell me what you are trying to build, fix, automate, or make easier to run, plus your timeline. I will respond with the next practical step.
